KNIGHT FORESTRY - UPDATE NOTES (v3)

DEPLOY ORDER (nothing goes offline if you follow this)
1. Upload ALL files in this folder to your site (theme.css and theme.js too).
   The pages work with the OLD Apps Script until step 2; they just show "password protection is OFF".
2. Update the quote/sync Apps Script WITHOUT changing its link:
   open the script -> paste quote-apps-script-v3.gs over the old code -> Save ->
   Deploy -> Manage deployments -> pencil -> Version: "New version" -> Deploy.
   NEVER choose "New deployment" - that creates a new link and every device would point at a dead one.
3. On one device: Settings -> Company Sync -> Save to Company. You will be asked to create the company password.
4. On every other device: Settings -> Company Sync -> Sync to Company.
   A brand-new device: open the app, paste the one Company Sync link on the Connect screen.

ROLLBACK
- Pages: put the previous index.html / scale.html / yards.html / theme.css back.
- Script: Deploy -> Manage deployments -> pencil -> pick the previous version -> Deploy.
- Saved prices keep working either way: "billed" fields keep their old names (pricePerTonne, hourly, fuelPrice).

NOT CHANGED ON PURPOSE
- dispatch.html, invoices.html, sw.js, manifest.json and the other .gs files are untouched.
- apps-script-code.gs is an OLD script. Do not paste it over the live one (it has no dispatch/billing support).

=== v8: ONE company script + one sheet (company-apps-script.gs) ===
Upload ALL site files first (nav.js, every page). Nothing changes until a device connects to the new link:
devices still on the separate scripts keep working exactly as before.

MOVING KNIGHT (once, from one phone that is set up and synced):
 1. script.google.com -> New project -> paste company-apps-script.gs -> Save ->
    Deploy -> New deployment -> Web app -> Execute as: Me -> Who has access: Anyone -> Deploy -> authorize.
 2. Quote -> Settings -> Company Sync -> "Move to one company sheet": paste the new link, the company password,
    and (optional) the Drivers admin password -> Move my company.
    Copies loads, tickets, inventory, drivers + passcodes + shifts, settings, company info and logo.
    The old scripts and sheets are NOT changed (keep them as a backup).
 3. Other devices: next time the app opens they are asked for the company password to switch.
    Driver-only phones: Drivers -> paste the new link (passcodes stay the same).
 After moving: the Drivers admin password is the company password. Changing the company password
 disconnects every device until it enters the new one.

A NEW COMPANY (e.g. a brother), on a fresh phone or after Quote -> Settings -> Switch company:
 1. Same as step 1 above, in THEIR Google account.
 2. Open the app, paste their link on the Connect screen -> "Set up your company" (name, password, logo...).
 3. Add pickup locations and trucks in Quote -> Settings -> Save to Company. GST/WCB/e-transfer in Billing -> Settings.
 Their sheet starts empty; nothing from Knight is copied. A phone that holds another company's data
 warns and clears it before connecting.

Every night the script saves a copy of the sheet in Drive ("Company Data backups", 14 kept) and removes GPS
trail / driver logs older than 12 months. Loads, tickets and invoices are never removed automatically.
The Log tab records every change (for the statistics page later).
